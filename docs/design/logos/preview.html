klinote.one · round 2 — not a letterhead

1 · Block

An ink tile. White K. This is a Mac app in the dock, not a law firm. Works at 16px and 1024px.

2 · Wordmark

Lowercase, tight, SF Pro. No rule, no serif, no domain line. The name is the mark.

3 · Stamp

A filing stamp on the letter — ready to copy, not a hospital cross. Double ring so it holds when small.

4 · Insert

A caret. The job is paste into the record. Almost too clever. Included because the last round was not clever enough.

5 · Gutter

Two rules and one block. Note column, margin, evidence. The most abstract. The one designers pick and clients don’t.
