## The discomfort

You already know how the afternoon ends.

The last patient leaves. The inbox does not. The note for the 10:30 is still a blank SOAP, and the 11:00 is in the waiting room. Cloud scribes will take the audio — and the contract, the DPA, the per-seat fee, and a recording of this room sitting on someone else’s disk.

You did not train so you could negotiate that.

---

## What you get

**A draft in your structure.** SOAP, physiotherapy, psychology, dentistry, veterinary. The sections you actually file, not a transcript dump.

**Every sentence has a source.** Click a line. See the words that produced it. If it cannot be placed, it sits in the margin. Nothing is quietly dropped.

**Copy, then paste.** There is no Epic button. There is a note you can put on the clipboard and into the record you already use. That is the path on purpose.

**The machine does not sign.** You do. A draft is a draft until you say otherwise.

---

## How it works

1. **Hold record.** A strip on the desk, visible to the patient. Pause if you need to. The audio does not leave this Mac.
2. **Read the letter.** Subjective, objective, assessment, plan — or the template your discipline uses. Evidence in the margin.
3. **Copy the note.** Paste into the record. File it there, not here.

Three minutes of attention after the consult. Not an evening of it.

---

## Why it is not a cloud scribe

Cloud scribes are good at notes. They are also a second institution in the room.

Klinote runs on the Mac on the desk. The speech engine downloads once. After that, the network is not involved. There is no account. There is no per-clinician seat at a vendor who holds the audio.

If your reason for refusing Nabla or Abridge is the contract, this is the product that reason was looking for.

## Who it is for

GPs, physiotherapists, psychologists, dentists, vets. One to three people in a practice. A Mac on the desk next to the record system. No IT department.

If you are happy sending consult audio to a vendor, you do not need this.

---

## What it is not

Not a diagnosis. Not a medical device. Not “your AI copilot”. Not an EHR. Not encrypted at rest yet — which is why this build is teaching-only.

It drafts documentation of a consult you already did. You decide what enters the record.

---

## FAQ

**Does audio leave the Mac?**
No. The only download is the speech engine, once. Notes and recordings are not uploaded.

**Does it type into my EHR?**
No. You copy the note and paste it. That is slower to demo and faster to trust.

**Can I use it with real patients?**
Not this build. Encryption at rest is not in yet. Teaching and your own voice only.

**What if it invents a finding?**
You will see the source line, or you will see that there isn’t one. Empty sections are marked missing. Unplaced speech sits in the margin. You still read the letter.

**Windows?**
No. Mac only.
