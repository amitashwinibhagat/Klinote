# Roadmap

Ordering principle: **who it is for, then whether they will capture a
session, then the smallest change that serves that job.** Canonical
positioning: `docs/product/POSITIONING.md`.

The previous roadmap gated “build the shell” on 3 paying GPs. The shell
exists. The GPs were never asked. That gate is retired. The new gate is
**3 paying therapists / psychologists / psychiatrists**, and it is allowed
to kill the microphone.

---

## Now — stop being a GP product

- [x] Engine, shell, Whisper, Quire, SQLCipher, sandbox, notarized 0.1.6
- [x] Recast: therapy / psych room, not “solo clinicians”
- [ ] Copy and PRODUCT-TRUTH: session, client, progress note
- [ ] Do not ship another dental / vet / physio template

## This month — conversations, not code

From `VALIDATION-PLAN.md` and `POSITIONING.md`:

- [ ] 10 conversations with therapists, psychologists, psychiatrists
- [ ] The kill question: **would you record a session if it never left this Mac?**
- [ ] Fair / expensive / prohibitive vs Upheal Free and Mentalyc $20
- [ ] 3 paid months, real sessions (record *or* dictate)

**Green:** they pay and they capture. → DAP/BIRP, 50-minute ASR if they record, process/progress split.
**Amber:** they pay for dictate/paste only. → that is the product; stop spending on the mic.
**Red:** they will not capture, or Upheal is fine. → stop.

Template cue fixes a paying therapist asks for are the only permitted code.

## Explicitly not planned

- Cloud anything.
- Diagnosis, alliance scores, treatment-plan AI, client-facing summaries.
- Dentistry, veterinary, physiotherapy as a product line.
- Epic / hospital EHR.
- Freemium to beat Upheal.
- Group-practice GTM.

## Kill criteria (do not negotiate)

- 5 of 10 will not capture a session (record *or* dictate). There is no job.
- Majority objection is “Upheal is free and good enough.” The segment is imaginary.
- Invented-finding rate on real sessions is not better than the cloud they fired.
- Reviewing the draft takes longer than typing.
